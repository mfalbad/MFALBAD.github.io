About the case

Hello! I'm Mustafa Al-Badri - an aspiring lifelong learner, I'm currently resitting my A-levels and using the time to build something real rather than just wait it out.

I'm drawn to the fields of biology and technology in equal measure - the kind of curiosity that wants to understand how living systems and built systems both work - alongside a genuine passion for the culinary arts (yum!), where the same precision and creativity show up on a plate instead of a website.

This site is where I'm documenting that process publicly: for anyone following along on this very ambitious journey of mine.
